Set it up

One command

curl -fsSL https://chorus-cli.web.app/install.sh | bash

Chorus opens and finds the CLIs you already use. Click New chat, pick an agent, and work as usual.

Then run chorus desktop: Chorus opens in your browser and finds the CLIs you already use.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and at least one of Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity, signed in with your own plan. Nothing else to install: Chorus brings its own runtime.

Needs Linux (x86_64 or arm64, glibc) and at least one of Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity, signed in with your own plan. Nothing else to install: Chorus brings its own runtime. On Linux there is no app window yet: Chorus runs in your browser or your terminal.

Chorus runs on macOS and Linux. On Windows, run the command in WSL.

Why

Several AI CLIs, one messy desk. Chorus tidies it.

Each CLI is its own island. Moving work between them means pasting context by hand, and you lose track of what each agent is doing.

How it works

One memory. Every agent.

Chorus records every chat as you work. Each agent reads that shared memory through the Chorus MCP server, so whichever agent you open next knows what the others did.

Recorded, not asked

A handoff works even when the first agent is out of quota: nothing has to come from it.

Across projects

Search every chat in Memory and continue any of them with another agent.

On your Mac

Your chats stay in one file on your computer.

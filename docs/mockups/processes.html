What changes, and why

Decided 2026-11-04: a process is a chain of actions, built with ＋. Plan & build comes with Chorus as one piece: you use it whole or not at all. Read against chorus-core src/pro/runner/index.ts and src/pro/playbook/index.ts.

Today vs this proposal

TodayProposal
A horizontal chain on a zoom-and-drag canvas, read-only; you edit an action in the teammate editorA vertical list you edit right there; ＋ between rows picks what comes next. No canvas, no dragging
Create plan, Do step, Review changes and Resolve conflict are four actions with their own next, ask-first and if-it-fails settingsThey are one piece, Plan & build, with a lock. Inside, it shows what happens, read-only. You change who does it in Teammates
Reproduce bug → Do step (a Do step with no task and no step)Reproduce bug → Plan & build: the Planner turns the failing test into a task
"You: Approve" is a small chip on the arrow"You decide" is its own row, with its answers
"Start step 1""Start: claude-worker-1 does step 1", with a numbered list of what Start does
A running task: a chip per step in the side panelThe same Plan & build stages, with times, chats and send-backs

Why Plan & build is locked

  • In the runner it isn't a chain already: afterStep() calls review() whatever Do step's next says, and Do step repeats once per plan step.
  • Some of today's settings do nothing (Do step's next); some break it (taking the ask-first off Review changes removes Accept).
  • One sentence explains Start: "Plan & build gives each step of the task to its Worker". The detail is shown, not configured.

Why not a free canvas

  • The engine is a chain: one next, an ask-first, pass or fail. A canvas promises branches and joins it doesn't have.
  • Loose boxes, loops and two arrows out of one box each need a rule and an error message: the n8n cost.
  • ＋ is the same choice as "What happens next?", put where you're looking.

What chorus-core needs

  • Hide buildTask, reviewChanges and resolveConflict from the picker, the Then select and a teammate's Actions list; saveAction refuses next / gate / onFail / kind on them.
  • reproduceBug.next: buildTask → createPlan.
  • ＋ after Plan & build: accepted() starts the next action (stored on the piece, e.g. reviewChanges.next, unused today). Today an accepted task stops there.
  • The list, the ⚙ lines and the Run view only read what is stored now.

Open questions

  • Can you still edit the instructions of the actions inside (for example how the Planner reviews), as an advanced setting in Teammates?
  • Plan & build anywhere in a chain, or only as its last piece?
  • Its name: "Plan & build", "Task", "Build it"?
  • Run views: Worker names (claude-worker-1), or "Workers"?

When you press Start
  1. Task #12 moves to Doing. (If another task in cake-shop is already running, #12 waits for it and starts by itself after.)
  2. Chorus opens a new chat for C claude-worker-1 in ~/projects/cake-shop: Claude Code · sonnet · low effort · asks before commands.
  3. Its first message is step 1's brief.
    You are claude-worker-1, doing one step of #12 "Add a login page". Why: Users can't sign in yet · Done when: a user signs in with email and password Step 1 of 3: Add the login form How to do it: A form with email, password and a Sign in button… How to check it: npm test -- login passes Finish: when your work is done, run its check, then call the chorus tool action_done with outcome "pass" or "fail"…
  4. You can watch, or step in, in that chat. It is listed under the task, and in the sidebar.
  5. When its check passes, step 2 starts in a new chat with X codex-worker-1. If a check fails, it goes back to the same Worker, at most 2 times, then to Needs you.
  6. After step 3, P Planner reviews the changes. Then you accept, or send it back.

Chorus never commits: the work stays in the folder for you.

What changes, and why

This builds on today's page and the teammate editor (chorus-core src/pro/ui/app.js), and on the runner, which stays as it is.

Today vs this proposal

Why not a free canvas

  • The runner is a chain: one next, a gate, and pass/fail. A canvas promises branches and joins the engine doesn't have.
  • Unconnected nodes, cycles and two arrows leaving one box each need a rule and an error.
  • "＋ pick what comes next" is the same choice as the "What happens next?" select, placed where you look.

Two kinds of action

  • Teammate actions (Create plan, Do step, Review changes, yours) are rows. They're the only thing you add.
  • Chorus actions (create the task, move its state, queue it, send it back, close its chats) aren't rows. They follow from the rows and appear as ⚙ lines.
  • You decide is the one non-teammate row, because it's where you take part.

What the engine needs

  • Nothing for the list, the ⚙ lines or the run view: they read next, gate, onFail and the event table.
  • "＋ You decide" between rows maps to gate on the row above.
  • Optional later: onFail: 'to:<action>' for "if it fails, go to…". Not in this mockup.

Open questions

  • "Workers" or the names (claude-worker-1)? The mockup uses the names in a run and "Workers" in the process.
  • Should Resolve conflict (parallel only) always show as a dashed row, or only when "In parallel" is picked?
  • Does an action keep living under a teammate, or under the process with a "who" picker? The mockup does both: the row has a Who select.

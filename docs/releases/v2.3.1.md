# v2.3.1

Released 2026-11-13, tagged on `v2.3.1-preview.1` (commit `b7d61b0`), as a hotfix on the owner's word ("do hot fix NOW").
Install: `curl -fsSL https://chorus-cli.web.app/install.sh | sh`.

## Why
2.3.0 could not start: its production build bundled an older chorus-core than its previews were tested with
([failure note](../failures/2026-11-13-production-built-old-core.md)). 2.3.1 is the same app code as 2.3.0
([v2.3.0](v2.3.0.md)), built after chorus-core `main` was brought up to `dev`.

## Evidence
- Every job of the production build succeeded, including the paid module.
- The macOS bundle was downloaded, checked against `SHA256SUMS`, and started with a temporary home: `chorus serve` printed its
  address and answered.
- After the website was updated: `release.json` says 2.3.1, each download answers 200, and a plain install into temporary folders
  installs 2.3.1 and starts.

## Not yet checked
- The same list as [v2.3.0](v2.3.0.md).
- The Linux bundles were built and their checksums verified, but not started.

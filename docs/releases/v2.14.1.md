# v2.14.1

Hotfix, released 2026-11-25 on the owner's word ("Approve v2.14.1"). Built from `hotfix/pro-update-refresh`, branched from the
`v2.14.0` tag; tagged on its head 721b071 (the fix and the version). chorus-core `main` 235f92f carries the loader fix (chorus-core
PR #83, issue #82). Install: `curl -fsSL https://chorus-cli.web.app/install.sh | sh`.

## What
- **After an app update, the paid module for the new version arrives without a restart** (chorus-core #82). After the update to
  2.14.0 the first launch's one request for the new paid module (4 s) did not land and nothing asked again, so the old module ran on
  (no QR code on Add a device) until a manual recheck. Now the loader asks again behind the running app (at once, then 30 s, 2, 10
  and 30 min) until it lands, runs the new module at once, and logs every miss to `desktop.log`.
- The window reloads its paid screens when that happens: at once when it just opened or is hidden, else the next time it is hidden,
  so nothing being typed is lost.

# v2.4.0

Released 2026-11-13, tagged on `v2.4.0-preview.1` (commit `bf598f8`), released on the owner's word ("when you done just release
production no need preview"). Install: `curl -fsSL https://chorus-cli.web.app/install.sh | sh`.

## What
Every place that shows a waiting item (an agent's question, a command prompt, a plan approval, a check, a chat to restore) has the
same controls: its answer buttons, Skip on a question, and delete behind the one warning. Delete is new on the card docked in a
chat, on the Approve / Deny line, on the Portal's Needs you, on a phone's Home and on the Restore card. A deleted plan approval
or check is only taken off the lists: it still waits on its task, which keeps its buttons. In 2.3.1 deleting one from Approvals
left the task with nothing to approve.

## Evidence
- `npm test` 237 pass of 238 (the one failure, `serve --host <name>`, fails on the machine it was run on before these changes too)
  and typecheck is clean; chorus-core 287 pass, chorus-admin 60 pass. Every job of the production build succeeded; it bundled
  chorus-core `main`, brought up to `dev` before the tag.
- Proof stages with stand-in agents: the host's 7 of 7; chorus-core's waiting-items 6 of 6 and planner-loop 8 of 8 against this host.
- The macOS bundle was downloaded, checked against `SHA256SUMS` and started with a temporary home before publishing: `chorus serve`
  printed its address, the page answered 200 and the state API answered. After the website was updated, a plain install into
  temporary folders installed 2.4.0 and started the same way; `release.json` says 2.4.0 and every download answers 200.

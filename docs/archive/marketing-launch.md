# Marketing & Go-To-Market Plan — Chorus

## 1. Positioning & Value Proposition

> *"Build apps with the world's best AI agents without ever touching a terminal."*

Chorus is not marketed as a "developer terminal multiplexer". It is marketed as **the visual control center for vibe coders, solo founders, and non-technical creators.**

---

## 2. Short-Form Video Strategy (TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts)

### Script 1: "The Terminal Horror vs. Chorus" (45 Seconds)
- **0–5s**: Screen capture of 3 black terminal windows scrolling rapidly with red error messages. Creator looks bewildered.
- **5–15s**: Open Chorus. Clean, dark-mode window with 3 tabs: `Claude`, `AGY`, `Codex`.
- **15–30s**: "Watch this: I ask Claude to build the login screen. Then I switch tabs to AGY—and AGY already knows what Claude did without me typing anything."
- **30–40s**: A dangerous command is intercepted: Chorus pops up a clean card: *"Claude wants to update your database. [Approve] [Reject]"*. Creator clicks Approve.
- **40–45s**: "Stop being afraid of AI coding. Download Chorus today with a 14-day free trial."

### Script 2: "Conducting a 3-Agent Symphony" (30 Seconds)
- Fast-paced demonstration showing `@agy` asking `@codex` to run tests via the shared MCP bridge while the user watches from the visual dashboard.

## 3. Launch Channels

1. **Social Media Video Ads**:
   - Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram Reels) targeting interests: Vibe Coding, Indie Hackers, No-Code, AI Tools, Solopreneurs.
   - TikTok organic videos demonstrating app creation in 5 minutes.
2. **Product Hunt Launch**:
   - Day 1 launch targeting the "AI Tools" and "Developer Tools" leaderboards.
3. **KOL Gifting**:
   - Give 100 free lifetime licenses to YouTube creators who teach AI web app development.

# Product Concept — Chorus

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy

AI coding is undergoing a massive shift:
- First wave: Single chatbot inside a code editor (Copilot, Cursor).
- Second wave: Autonomous CLI agents running in terminals (`agy`, `claude`, `codex`).
- **The Chorus wave**: A unified, friendly **"Safety Cockpit"** that unites the world's best CLI engines in one window, allowing non-technical creators to direct them like an orchestra without touching a terminal.

## 2. Target Audience: "Vibe Coders" & Non-Technical Builders

Chorus is deliberately built for:
- **Solo Founders & Entrepreneurs**: People building SaaS, tools, or landing pages without a technical co-founder.
- **Marketers, PMs & Designers**: Non-engineers who want to build internal tools or prototypes with AI.
- **Vibe Coders**: Developers and hobbyists who guide AI via high-level intent rather than manual syntax editing.

### Why Existing Tools Fail This Audience:
1. **Terminal Intimidation**: Raw command lines, fast-scrolling ANSI codes, and obscure bash errors trigger anxiety.
2. **Fear of Machine Harm**: Users worry: *"Did Claude just delete my database or personal files?"*
3. **Model Siloing**: Switching from Claude to AGY requires copying and pasting endless context snippets.

## 3. Brand Identity: Why "Chorus"?

- **Phonetics**: `/ˈkɔːr.əs/` — smooth, elegant, and civilized compared to harsh technical jargon.
- **The Metaphor**:
  - `claude` is the detailed soloist (deep reasoning & code writing).
  - `agy` is the architectural soloist (multi-agent planning & deep tools).
  - `codex` is the virtuoso (high-speed logic & testing).
  - **Chorus** brings them onto one stage to sing in harmony on the exact same song.
  - **The User** is the **Conductor** (Nhạc trưởng), directing the performance and approving each crescendo.
